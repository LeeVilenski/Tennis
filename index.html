Press early. Tap before the ball reaches Lee. He winds up and swings when it arrives. The longer the wind-up (up to a second), the harder the hit.

Aim: hold left or right (on the keys or the stick) at contact to pick a side. Up hits deep, down plays a drop shot.

Tennis in brief

Serve overhead, diagonally into the service box. Two tries. J tosses for a flat serve (fast, risky), K for a kick serve (slower, safer, bounces high). Press again at the top of the toss: early goes long, late goes in the net. Big serve (first serve only): hold J through the toss and let go at the top. Much faster, but the timing window is tighter.

Keep it in. Your shot must land inside the lines. This is doubles, so the outer tramlines count, except on the serve.

One bounce. Play the ball before it bounces twice, or volley it before it bounces (not on the return of serve).

Leave it. If a ball is flying long or wide, don't touch it. It's your point.

No walls. Lee has noticed, and he has opinions.

No-ad scoring: off by default. Switch it on to play deuce as a single deciding point.
